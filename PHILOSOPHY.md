# ColorWizard Desktop Philosophy

Color work should be stable, private, and durable.

ColorWizard Desktop is built as a small, owned tool:

- Your projects are files and folders, not records in someone else's cloud.
- Core workflows remain available offline.
- The app avoids service lock-in and recurring backend burden.
- Features are chosen for reliability first, then expansion.

This keeps the product understandable over years, not quarters.
